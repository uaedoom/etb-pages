Privacy Policy

English Tracing Book for Kids
Effective date: 2 October 2026

We take children's privacy seriously. This app is designed so that nothing about your child is collected, shared or sold.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for a name, email address, location, photos, contacts or any other personal information, and it has no accounts or sign-in.

What stays on the device

Your child's stars, stickers and sound on/off setting are saved only on this device so that progress is remembered. We cannot see this information and it is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes it.

Advertising, tracking and third parties

The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party software that collects data. It does not need an internet connection to work, and it makes no network requests.

Children

The app is made for young children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, there is no data from children to protect, share or delete.

Purchases and links

The app has no in-app purchases and no links to outside websites.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
