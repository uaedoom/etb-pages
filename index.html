Quick answers

How do I trace a letter? Tap a letter, then put a finger (or Apple Pencil) on the glowing numbered badge and follow the little hand. Draw each stroke in the order of the numbers.

The voice is silent. Check the iPad volume and the silent switch, and make sure the sound button on the main menu is on (the red speaker).

How do I start over? Open Settings (the gear button on the main menu) and choose "Reset all stars".

Where are the Shapes and Sounds cards? Swipe the row of cards on the main menu to see more.
